Frequently Asked Questions

Where in Melbourne do you provide garden maintenance?

We work in gardens across Melbourne, including Blackburn North, Hawthorn, Eltham, Donvale, Surrey Hills and Camberwell. Contact us to confirm availability in your suburb.

What gardening services do you offer?

Our services include lawn care, specialist plant pruning, hedge trimming and topiary for Melbourne gardens.

When should shrubs and garden plants be pruned?

The right time depends on the plant species, its flowering cycle and the result you want. We use appropriate seasonal pruning techniques for each plant.

Can you maintain formal hedges and topiary?

Yes. We shape and maintain formal hedges and topiary, including ongoing trimming to keep growth neat and healthy.
